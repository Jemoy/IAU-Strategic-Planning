# Proposed Academic Organizational Structure

**Indiana Aerospace University** (formerly Indiana School of Aeronautics)
**Proposal for Restructuring of Academic Units**
Prepared in response to the CHEDRO VII Onsite Monitoring and Evaluation of May 22, 2024

---

## 1. Rationale

The May 2024 monitoring identified a single shared dean across all programs, program chairs without master's degrees or with degrees outside their discipline, and programs without designated chairs. The proposed structure consolidates all programs into three schools, each with a qualified full-time head and discipline-specialized program chairs, under a Vice President for Academic Affairs who also serves as Dean of All Schools.

## 2. Proposed Structure

```
President / represented by the Chief Executive Officer
└── Vice President for Academic Affairs / Dean of All Schools
    ├── School of Aerospace and Aviation
    │   ├── Program Chair, BS Aerospace Engineering
    │   ├── Program Chair, BS Aircraft Maintenance Technology
    │   ├── Program Chair, BS Avionics Technology
    │   └── Program Chair, BS Aviation Technology
    ├── School of Management
    │   ├── Program Chair, BS Airline Management
    │   ├── Program Chair, BS Hospitality Management
    │   └── Program Chair, BS Tourism Management
    └── School of Education and the Arts
        ├── Program Chair, Bachelor of Elementary Education
        └── Program Chair, Bachelor of Secondary Education (English, Mathematics)
```
