# Faculty SWOT Analysis and TOWS Matrix
**In-Service Training (INSET) Report**
*Prepared: September 9, 2026*

---

## 1. Overview

Four faculty groups conducted a SWOT analysis of the institution during the INSET workshop. This report consolidates their outputs, identifies recurring themes, and converts them into actionable strategies through a TOWS matrix. A prioritization table is provided for the closing session and action planning.

### 2.5 Observations

| Observation | Detail |
|-------------|--------|
| Facilities split | Groups 3 and 4 cite "state-of-the-art / improving facilities" as a strength while Groups 1 and 2 cite poor facilities as a weakness. This likely reflects a gap between specialized aviation labs and general classrooms. |
| Safety cluster | Lab hazards (W6), lax PPE use (T7), and calamity risk (T5) form a safety theme that cuts across categories. |
| Untapped differentiator | The airline management program is listed as a strength (S6) but also as an opportunity for brand positioning (O6), suggesting it is under-marketed. |
| Documentation | Missing lab manual, graduate tracer, and archiving (W5) directly weaken audit readiness (T2). |

## 3. TOWS Matrix

| | **Opportunities (O)** | **Threats (T)** |
|---|---|---|
| **Strengths (S)** | **SO Strategies** — use strengths to seize opportunities | **ST Strategies** — use strengths to defend against threats |
| **Weaknesses (W)** | **WO Strategies** — fix weaknesses by exploiting opportunities | **WT Strategies** — minimize weaknesses to avoid threats |

### 3.1 SO Strategies (Strengths + Opportunities)

| Code | Strategy | Factors Used |
|------|----------|--------------|
| SO1 | Package the A320 mock cabin, aircraft, and industry-competent instructors into short technical courses for industry partners and MRO firms, with the Airbus partnership as anchor | S1, S2, O1 |
| SO2 | Leverage CAAP compliance and government connections to secure priority internship and hiring pipelines with airports and airlines | S3, O2, O5 |
| SO3 | Market the unique airline management program and 100% LET passer rate as headline differentiators to capture the growing aviation applicant pool | S6, S10, S12, O2, O6 |
| SO4 | Use the media bureau, global partnerships, and rising enrollment to launch international exchange and exposure programs | S5, S9, S12, O4 |

### 3.2 WO Strategies (Weaknesses + Opportunities)

| Code | Strategy | Factors Used |
|------|----------|--------------|
| WO1 | Negotiate equipment donations, lab sponsorship, or shared facilities as conditions of new industry and MRO partnership agreements | W2, O1 |
| WO2 | Use CHED monitoring and curriculum review cycles to enforce prerequisites, produce laboratory manuals, and formalize documentation and archiving | W5, W10, O3, O7 |
| WO3 | Attach a graduate tracer study to partnership and scholarship programs so employers help track alumni outcomes | W5, O1, O8 |
| WO4 | Channel growing enrollment revenue into a phased facilities plan: ventilation and air-conditioning, canteen and CR sanitation, student lounge, e-library access | W1, W3, W4, O2 |

### 3.3 ST Strategies (Strengths + Threats)

| Code | Strategy | Factors Used |
|------|----------|--------------|
| ST1 | Formalize QMS/SMS and CAAP compliance into audit-ready systems so regulatory changes become a competitive edge over LGU colleges and TESDA | S3, S8, T1, T2 |
| ST2 | Use the LMS to build continuity-of-learning protocols for class suspensions and extend IAU-DRRMO coverage to lab hazard and PPE enforcement | S7, S11, T5, T7 |
| ST3 | Deploy competent instructors and industry links in retention efforts (mentoring, career pathing) to counter student piracy and negative online reviews | S1, S3, T8 |
| ST4 | Position aviation management and human-factors skills as AI-resilient in recruitment and curriculum messaging | S6, T9, T10 |

### 3.4 WT Strategies (Weaknesses + Threats)

| Code | Strategy | Factors Used |
|------|----------|--------------|
| WT1 | Conduct an immediate safety audit covering lab hazards, PPE enforcement, and campus security | W6, T5, T7 |
| WT2 | Invest in cybersecurity, data privacy compliance, and a reliable payment processing system before an incident or audit forces it | W11, T3 |
| WT3 | Establish scholarship and flexible payment schemes to protect enrollment during downturns and against lower-cost competitors | W11, T1, T4, T6 |
| WT4 | Run structured faculty development to close instructor competency gaps so competitor schools cannot outpace on quality | W7, T1 |
